# LEK-9 LED Experimenter’s Kit

The LEK-9 is a collection of nine assembled LED circuit boards supplied as a 3 × 3-inch V-scored panel. Keep the panel together or snap the boards apart to build your own experiments.

## Documentation

- [Download the LEK-9 User Manual](LED_Driver_Experimenters_Kit_Manual.pdf)

The user manual includes circuit descriptions, specifications, connection diagrams, and hands-on experiments.
