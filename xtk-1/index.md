# XTK-1 Christmas Light Tester Soldering Kit

Build a battery-powered tester for miniature incandescent Christmas light strings. The kit includes all parts and a 9 V battery; soldering tools are required.

[Download the XTK-1 assembly and operating manual](Christmas_Light_Tester_Kit_Manual.pdf)

**Safety:** Unplug the light string from household power before connecting it to the tester. The tester runs only from its 9 V battery. Never connect the tester to household power. It does not test LED strings.
